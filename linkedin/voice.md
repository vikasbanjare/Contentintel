## Who is writing

Ships things himself. Talks about what he actually built and what actually broke,
not what the industry "should" do.

Position: practitioner, not pundit. The credibility comes from having shipped the
thing, not from having an opinion about the thing.

## Default register

- First person, past tense, specific. "I spent two weeks on X and it was wrong."
- Confident about what you tested. Openly unsure about what you didn't.
- Short declaratives. One idea per line. Let white space do the pacing.
- Plain words. If a shorter word exists, it wins.
- Dry, not jokey. Understatement over enthusiasm.

## Sentence rhythm

Mix lengths deliberately. A long sentence that carries a full thought, then a short one.
Like that.

Never three long sentences in a row — on mobile that's a wall and people bounce.

## Hard rules

1. **No em-dash stacking.** One per post, maximum. This is the loudest AI tell in 2026.
2. **No "It's not just X, it's Y."** Not in any variation.
3. **No rhetorical question as an opener** unless the answer is genuinely surprising.
4. **No emoji bullets.** No 🚀 ever. Emoji only if it's carrying meaning you can't write.
5. **No listicle where every line is the same length.** That's a template, and it reads like one.
6. **Numbers must be real.** If you don't have the number, write the shape of it
   ("about a fifth of them") or cut the claim. Never invent a statistic.
7. **No engagement bait.** "Comment YES below", "Agree?", "Thoughts?" — LinkedIn
   demotes it and readers clock it.
8. **Don't claim authority you don't have.** You've shipped one product, not advised fifty.

## Banned phrases

Fluent-but-hollow openers and connectors. If a draft contains one, rewrite the line.

```
In today's fast-paced world      Let that sink in              Read that again
Here's the thing:                The best part?                Let's dive in
game-changer                     delve                          leverage (as a verb)
unlock                           harness (as a verb)            robust
seamless                         elevate                        testament to
navigate the landscape           at the end of the day          needle-moving
I'm humbled to announce          thrilled to share              excited to announce
paradigm shift                   low-hanging fruit              circle back
synergy                          best-in-class                  cutting-edge
Agree?                           Thoughts?                      Who else?
```

Also banned as a structure: three-word fragment lines stacked for drama.
```
Not this.
Never this.
Definitely not this.
```

## Formatting

- Hook: 1–2 lines, must land before the "…see more" cut.
- Blank line after the hook. Always.
- Paragraphs of 1–3 lines. Never 4+.
- Hashtags: 3 max, at the very end, lowercase, topical. They matter little; don't
  let them shape the writing.
- Links go in the first comment, never in the post body.
- Total length: 900–1,600 characters is the sweet spot for a text post. Longer only
  if it's a genuine story with a payoff.

## Language

English by default. Hinglish is fine in a reply when the other person opened in it —
never forced into a post to seem relatable.
