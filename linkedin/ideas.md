# Idea Bank

Thoughts captured before they're posts. `/li-idea` appends here in two seconds.
`/li-mine` adds angles found in your git history. `/li-daily` and `/li-week` read
from here when you arrive empty.

**Status:** `raw` — captured, no angle yet · `ready` — has an angle and evidence ·
`used` — posted, with the date · `dead` — decided against, kept so it isn't re-raised.

Prune `used` and `dead` entries once a quarter. Everything else is fuel.

## Entry format

```markdown
### One-line title, stated as the claim
- **captured** 2026-09-21 · **pillar** building · **status** raw
- **raw** what you actually said, verbatim, however messy
- **angle** which format from formats.md, once you know
- **evidence** the number, commit, screenshot or story that backs it
- **needs** what's missing before this can be a post
```

Only `captured`, `pillar`, `status` and `raw` are required at capture time. The rest
gets filled in when the idea is worked up. An idea with no `evidence` is not ready —
that's the honest gate, and it's why `raw` exists as a holding pen.
