# People

Who you're actually building relationships with. `/li-engage` reads this before
drafting and updates it after, so a reply can pick up where the last one left off
instead of treating a familiar face as a stranger.

This is the difference between 500 comments and five relationships. Comments are
activity; relationships are what turn into users, collaborators and work.

**Keep it small.** 20–40 people, max. A list of 300 is a spreadsheet nobody reads.
If someone's gone cold for three months and nothing's open, delete them — the
history isn't worth the noise.

**Privacy:** this is your own note-taking about public professional activity. Don't
record anything from a DM they'd consider private, anything about their employment
that isn't public, or anything you'd be embarrassed for them to read. Assume one
day they might.

## Entry format

```markdown
### Name — linkedin.com/in/handle
- **who** what they do, roughly what audience, why they're worth knowing
- **temperature** warm | known | watching
- **history**
  - 2026-08-14 — commented on their evals post re: retry logic; they replied asking how we handle partial failures
  - 2026-09-02 — they commented on my autopsy post
- **open** the thing you owe them, or the thread worth reviving. Blank if nothing.
- **avoid** anything that went badly, or a topic to stay off
```

`open` is the field that earns this file's existence. It's what turns "I should
engage more" into a specific next action.
