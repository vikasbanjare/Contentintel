# Thumbnail pattern analysis — Batch 3 (n=89)

**Set:** 89 thumbnails (incl. the 15 from Batch 1). Single channel: **GrowthX
"Wireframe"** — Indian business/startup/finance case-study explainers, English,
Indian audience. Highly templated → high-confidence patterns.
**Date:** 2026-06-03. Contact sheets saved as `sheet_01..08.png`.
**Caveat:** still no CTR/view labels, so this characterises the proven *house
style*, not which variant wins. Need winner labels to rank variants.

## The dominant formula (~70/89)
Founder/CEO/known figure **cut out on the right third** (smiling, eye contact)
+ **text on the left/bottom-left** led by a **big ₹/Crore number** OR an
**"India's ___ KING/Empire"** line + **blue background** (bright sky-blue OR
navy grid with a growth graph) + a **curved hand-drawn arrow** to the subject or
a **tilted brand-logo badge** + small GrowthX/WIREFRAME logo + optional black
**"100K+ VIEWS"** pill top-right.

## Text patterns (the biggest new insight, n=89)
- **Big-number hook dominates (~55–65%):** 12,000 Cr, 1 Lakh Crore, ₹580 Cr,
  700 Cr, 1,400 Cr, 50,000 Cr, ₹2 Lakh Crore, 42,000 Cr, 58,000 Cr, 150 CR…
  The number is the largest element.
- **"India's ___" possessive frame (national identity):** India's Real
  E-Commerce KING, India's Logistics Genius, India's NEW Sauce King, India's
  SECRET Payment King, India's FAN KING, India's Burrito/Pencil/Cola/Delivery King.
- **Authority/dominance nouns, usually serif-ITALIC emphasis:** KING, *Genius*,
  EMPIRE, *Masterplan*, *Dominance*, *Disruption*, *Secret Strategy*, GAME CHANGER.
- **Conflict framing:** "The man who beat RELIANCE", "The man CRUSHING
  MakeMyTrip", "The Man Who Broke Business", "Crushing NETFLIX In India",
  "Defeating OLA & UBER?", "95% FAILED", "What Zuck Fears Most".
- **Two-line formula:** serif-italic hook line + bold-sans descriptor (or
  bold number + descriptor). One accent-colour word (red/green/blue) for emphasis.
- **Question hooks** (minority): "Why Indians Love Weddings?", "Tesla in India?".
- **Crown graphic** literalises "King" (#057 FAN KING, #058 Payment King).

## Layouts (adds to Batch-1 library)
- **NEW · Chart / data-viz hero:** a prominent line/area/bar chart with figures
  — INDIGO vs Air India 2023, FB 90M→2.9B, Paytm bar chart, "Layoffs" chart,
  Airbnb +/- comparison. A "finance/data" sub-style.
- **NEW · Triptych / 3-panel:** OIL | INTERNET | AI (#082); UPI vs eRupee logos.
- Reinforced: Versus/duel (Modi vs Trump, Samsung & Tesla, SVB), Product/object
  hero (sneaker "₹4,50,000??", scooter, tractor, cold-drink), Full-bleed face.
- **Contrasting outliers (used sparingly):** minimalist premium "It's not just a
  Phone/Car" (clean, negative space, no loud text); podcast/interview stills
  (#051, #060 "WTF? Spent ₹50,000 on a Date").

## Colour (adds to library)
- **NEW · Navy grid + growth graph** (dark blue grid, white/green up-line) — the
  "data/finance" look, very common.
- **NEW · Stock-chart green/red** (gains/losses, +/- figures).
- Plus Batch-1: Sky-blue growth, Brand-matched (Fevicol blue, Airbnb, Zerodha,
  Netflix red/black, pink weddings).

## Faces / draw
- Mostly **smiling/confident**; **serious/intense** for conflict/news; a few
  **shocked/curious** (price reveals).
- **Recognizable celebrity faces** used as the hook: Ambani, Musk, Zuckerberg,
  Ratan Tata, MrBeast, Modi/Trump, Virat Kohli.
- **Brand-logo badge + arrow** is a signature "what is this?" device.

## Headline frequencies (visual estimate, n≈89)
- Face present: ~80% · Curved arrow: ~70% · Big-number hook: ~55–65%
- "India's ___" / King-Empire authority frame: ~25–30%
- Growth-graph/chart motif in background: ~50%
