## The series template (all 35)
- **Recurring HOST** (same creator, beard) prominent on one side, often pointing/expressive. Sometimes SOLO, sometimes paired with a **guest** — a state CM or founder (Himanta/Assam, Stalin/TN, Naidu/AP, Mamata/WB, CMs of Arunachal, Rajasthan, etc.).
- **HERO WORDMARK "[PLACE] STARTUPS"**: place name in a theme colour and/or serif-italic, "STARTUPS" in bold white sans. Same wordmark every episode = instant series recognition.
- **State/region MAP silhouette** (theme-coloured), often with a small arrow/locator.
- **City skyline / landmark photo** background (Bengaluru, Hyderabad Charminar, Goa, Mumbai, Chennai Marina…).
- **Featured startup brand-logos** in a row on some (#009, #012, #022 Wow Momo, #018).
- Small **cube/box channel logo**; friendly/curious tone, host gesturing.

## Hook variants
- Plain series: "Punjab Startups", "Goa Startups", "Mizoram Startups"…
- Curiosity / contrarian: "THEY'RE SELLING AIR" (#008), "ASIA'S FIRST" (bullet train, #017), "Andhra SPACETECH Startup" (#019), "SILICON VALLEY OF INDIA" (Hyderabad vs Bengaluru, #030), "MORE THAN BENGALURU" (Delhi-NCR, #033), "Karnataka Startups THAT AREN'T BASED OUT OF BENGALURU" (#035).
