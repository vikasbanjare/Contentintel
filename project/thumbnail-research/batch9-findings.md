- **Editorial female creator** (sheets 1–2, #070): cinematic single-face portrait, serious/intense, dramatic lighting, minimal restrained type ("MY STORY", "The Real China", "Future of Work", "DARK TRUTH"). Premium/serious register.
- **Loud brand/business explainer**: shocked faces + brand product + "THE [BRAND] ___" wordmark ("THE SHAMPOO REVOLUTION", "THE TASTE OF INDIA", "Haldiram's Billionaire", "THE MOST UNDERRATED", "THE REALITY OF TATA").

- **Gen-Z / group** channel (#073 K-POP, #082 Christmas Special).

## Strong CROSS-CREATOR hooks (recur across channels)
- **SCAM / exposé frame:** "[X] SCAM", "DARK TRUTH", "THE REALITY OF [BRAND]", "BEST KEPT SECRET REVEALED". Very common.
- **"THE [BRAND/THING] [REVOLUTION/TASTE/REALITY]"** two-line wordmark with the brand product shown.
- **Versus / comparison:** NORTH vs SOUTH, 2018 vs 2023, Cost vs Price, "ARE WE FINISHED?".
- **Curiosity question:** "ARE YOU IN DANGER?", "WHAT DOES GEN-Z WANT?", "TOXIC PARENTS?", "WHY YOU WON'T SUCCEED", "HOW?".
- **Two emotional registers:** calm-confident (business) vs **shocked/exaggerated** (eyes wide, mouth open) for scams/shock topics — a contrast to the calmer niches seen earlier.
- **Brand-product prop** held/shown + logo; **yellow highlight box** recurs again.
