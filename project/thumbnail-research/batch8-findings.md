## The template (all 84)
- **Guest expert on the LEFT + host (often a woman) ± co-host on the RIGHT**, podcast **microphones**, dark navy background (often a faint green/red market chart).
- **Center-top text** with the key number/word in a **coloured highlight box/pill** (yellow numbers; blue/white pills for "1 FUND!", "RICH!", "FREE", "2 FUNDS").
- **SIGNATURE: a small reaction-word box** by the host — "HOW? / HOW?? / WTF? / OMG? / Really? / WHY?" — amplifies the curiosity gap.
- **Curved arrows**; sometimes a **verified-tweet/quote card** (Samir Arora, Shaily Gang…) for credibility; occasional **view-count badge** (1 MILLION+, 500K+).

## Recurring hook frames
- **Myth-bust / contrarian:** "Mutual Funds / SIPs CAN'T make you RICH! — HOW?", "F.I.R.E. IS WRONG!", "Indian Market is Dead?", "Holding Cash? Big Mistake!".
- **Minimalist promise:** "You Only Need This 1 FUND / 1 ASSET / 1 Sector / 2 FUNDS".
- **Personal-finance number hooks:** networth/corpus/SIP figures (₹1.5 Cr, ₹25 Crore, ₹10 Cr, ₹250cr, ₹1,50,00,000), "Financially FREE at 34/37/48", "Retire Early in 10 Years", "Passive Income ₹5 Lakh/Month".
- **Transformation:** "Started with ₹650 → Crorepati", "₹0 → ₹5Cr in 11 Years", before/after year+amount labels.
- **How-to / quote:** "How to Invest your first ₹50 Lakhs", "I am Financially Free at 48!".
