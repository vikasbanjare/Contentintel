## Extracted patterns (the repeatable formula)

### Layout
- **Signature archetype (~10/15): Founder/CEO cut-out + real brand scene + bottom-left text + curved arrow.**
  - Real founder, cut out cleanly, placed on the **right third**, smiling, eye contact.
  - Background = the company's **real store / product / logo / vehicle**, on sky-blue or brand-grid, with a **faint upward line-graph** (growth motif).
  - **Hand-drawn curved white arrow** from the number/text toward the subject or hero object — in ~12/15. This is the channel's signature "look here" device.
  - Text block anchored **bottom-left** (bottom-center for duo layouts).
- Secondary archetypes seen: **Text-dominant split** (Zudio), **Product hero** (sneaker on cash, Blinkit bag), **Versus/duel** (Apple vs Modi, BluSmart vs Uber), **Full-bleed face** (Mensa).

### Color
- **Bright sky-blue daylight (clouds) is dominant (~9/15)** — optimism / growth.
- Palette often **brand-matched** (Whole Truth pink, DOMS blue grid, Mensa warm cream).
- **Faint upward line-graph** behind the subject in most — instant "success" signal.
- High brightness + saturation on the focal brand asset; subject rim-lit/separated.

### Text & typography
- **The hook is a huge money number**: 900 Cr, 80,000 Cr, 12,000 Cr, 8000 Cr, ₹160 CR, 70,000 Cr, 600 Cr — ~8/15 lead with a ₹/Crore figure as the LARGEST element.
- **Two-line formula:** line 1 = number/hook in **serif italic** (premium) OR heavy sans; line 2 = descriptor in **bold sans** ("Cloud Kitchen Factory", "Masterplan").
- **Authority/dominance power words** — King / Empire / Kingdom — ~6/15 ("Delivery King", "Cola King", "Pencil KING", "Fashion Empire", "King of Profits").
- 3–5 words per block. White text + soft shadow on photos; black on light backgrounds; one accent-colour word for emphasis (Whole Truth "Healthy" red).

### Social proof / branding
- **"100K+/400K+ VIEWS" black pill top-right** (~5/15) — bandwagon proof.
- Small **channel logo top-left / bottom-right** — consistent, unobtrusive.
- Occasional **status badge** (red "SOLD" ribbon) for news-style stories.

### Emotion / psychology
- Faces are **smiling, confident, aspirational** — NOT shock/MrBeast-face. Calmer tone fits a business audience. Eye contact throughout.
- Versus/news stories use **serious/intense** faces.
- Curiosity gap: the subject is usually **unnamed in text** ("India's ___ King") → "who is this?"; the arrow reinforces it.

### Outliers / cautions
- **Blue Tokai** has no text and no channel branding (lower-res) — does not fit the template; treat as a different style.
