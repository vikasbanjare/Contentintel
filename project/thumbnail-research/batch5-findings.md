## Extracted patterns
- **Interview framing:** the GUEST (often a recognizable founder), frequently with the HOST — 1–2 real people in a photographic real setting or dark studio. NOT heavy graphic compositing (the GrowthX tell).
- **Big-number hook still dominant** (₹900Cr, ₹200Cr, 500 CRORES, ₹1300CR, ₹650 CRORES, $250M, ₹1 Cr/month) — number in an accent colour (yellow/green/red).
- **Text frames:** "HOW TO BUILD A ___?", "HOW [BRAND] BECAME ₹X?", "FROM ___ TO ___", first-person quote "I make ₹X selling ___", or a sharp metric ("106X oversubscribed", "90% market share").
- **ALL-CAPS bold sans**; **brand name highlighted in a coloured box** (SOULED, NOISE) — marker emphasis (shared with the stock niche).
- **Darker, photographic, premium** palette vs GrowthX's bright graphic grid.
- **Topic/brand props:** product (face wash, bottles), plane, drone, phone, storefront.
- Channel logo top-left; "Ft. [name]" credit.

## vs other niches
- Shares ₹Crore-number hook with GrowthX, but: interview/host+guest (not cut-out on a grid), photographic/premium (not bright-blue graphic), question/how-to & quote frames (not "India's ___ King").
- Shares marker-highlight emphasis with the stock-tips creator.
